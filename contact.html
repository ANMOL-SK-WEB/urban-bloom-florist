Frequently asked questions

Can I request particular flowers or colours?

Yes. Customers may request particular flowers or colours, but availability depends on the season and current supply. Suitable alternatives may be recommended.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Same-day delivery may be available for some orders placed early in the day, but it cannot be guaranteed.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements for gift add-ons?

Please provide relevant requirements when making an enquiry. Availability of suitable gift items may vary.

How much notice is required for wedding or event flowers?

Customers are encouraged to enquire at least three weeks before smaller events and considerably earlier for weddings or large events.

Do you provide regular flowers for businesses?

Yes. Weekly, fortnightly and other recurring arrangements can be discussed with Urban Bloom.

Can I order and pay through the website?

No. The website is for browsing products and services and submitting enquiries. Online payment is not currently provided.
